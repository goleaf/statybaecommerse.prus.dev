# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a collaborative, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone who interacts with this project. All participants—including maintainers, contributors, and community members—must follow the rules below in every project space (repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, and in-person events).

## Our standards

- Be inclusive and considerate. Value different perspectives, backgrounds, and skill levels.
- Communicate constructively. Assume positive intent, give actionable feedback, and focus on the work instead of individuals.
- Respect boundaries. Do not use sexualised language or imagery, and refrain from unwelcome advances of any kind.
- Keep interactions professional. Avoid personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Safeguard private information. Share credentials, customer data, or internal details only through approved, secure channels.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following actions are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation based on gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, religion, or any other protected characteristic.
- Threats of violence, stalking, or deliberate intimidation.
- Doxing or publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of meetings, issues, or discussions.
- Impersonation of others or misrepresentation of authority.

## Responsibilities & enforcement

- **Maintainers** must enforce this Code of Conduct, investigate reports promptly, and apply corrective actions ranging from informal warnings to permanent bans.
- **Contributors** should report violations via the private security channel listed in `SECURITY.md` or by emailing the core maintainers at `maintainers@prus.dev`.
- Reports should include as much context as possible (what happened, when, where, and who was involved). We will respect requests for confidentiality to the extent allowed by law.
- Consequences for violating this code may include temporary bans, revoking commit access, or escalations to employer or legal authorities if necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces—online and offline—and to anyone representing the project in an official capacity. Maintain these standards when using personal accounts that can reasonably be associated with the project.

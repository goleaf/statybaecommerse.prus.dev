# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please email us at [security@prus.dev](mailto:security@prus.dev). Include a detailed description of the issue, steps to reproduce, the potential impact, and any suggested mitigations. We encourage you to encrypt sensitive details using our PGP key when possible.

We ask researchers to avoid publicly disclosing vulnerabilities before we have had an opportunity to investigate and prepare a fix. Please provide us with a reasonable amount of time to respond before making any information public.

## Response Timeline

- **Acknowledgement:** We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 2 business days.
- **Initial Assessment:** A member of the team will provide an initial assessment or request for clarification within 5 business days of acknowledgement.
- **Remediation:** We aim to release a fix, mitigation, or public guidance within 30 calendar days of confirmation, depending on severity and complexity. If more time is required, we will keep you informed with regular updates until the issue is resolved.

Thank you for helping us keep our community safe.
